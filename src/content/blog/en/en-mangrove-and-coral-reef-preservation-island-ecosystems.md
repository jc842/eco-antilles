# Preserving Caribbean Coastlines: Mangrove Restorations & Coral Reef Protection

Mangrove forests act as the vital biological lungs and hurricane storm-surge buffers of Caribbean shorelines.

---

## 1. Natural Coastal Defense
Dense root systems trap sediment, preventing terrestrial soil runoff from smothering fringing coral reefs while dissipating wave energy during tropical cyclones.

## 2. Community Coral Nurseries
Micro-fragmentation and underwater nursery tables actively regenerate resilient staghorn and brain coral colonies along degraded barrier reefs.
