# Tropical Bioclimatic Architecture: Passive Natural Cooling

Designing with Caribbean trade winds (*alizés*) drastically reduces reliance on energy-intensive air conditioning.

---

## 1. Catching the Trade Winds
Orienting living spaces along the East-to-West axis channels cooling eastern ocean breezes through adjustable aluminum or hardwood louvers (*jalousies*).

## 2. Deep Shading Overhangs (*Varangues*)
Expansive covered verandas shield perimeter exterior walls from direct solar radiation, preventing thermal mass buildup throughout the peak afternoon hours.
