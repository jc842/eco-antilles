# Solar Power in the Caribbean: Photovoltaic Sizing & Island Autonomy

Tropical island grids face high electricity generation costs and vulnerability to extreme weather events. Rooftop solar power represents the definitive path toward household energy resilience.

---

## 1. Cyclone-Resistant Solar Mounting (Wind Zone 5)
- **Reinforced Aluminum Racking**: Heavy-duty extruded rails certified to resist wind gusts up to 260 km/h.
- **Stainless Steel A4 Fasteners**: Marine-grade 316 stainless bolts to prevent galvanic corrosion from marine salt spray.

## 2. Battery Storage: LiFePO4 Chemistry
Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) batteries outperform older lead-acid banks with over 6,000 charge cycles, operating safely at tropical ambient temperatures without thermal runaway risk.
