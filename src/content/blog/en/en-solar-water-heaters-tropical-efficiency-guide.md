# Solar Thermal Water Heaters: Thermosiphon Systems & Payback Analysis

In sunny tropical territories, heating domestic water with electricity is an unnecessary economic drain.

---

## 1. The Thermosiphon Principle
Operating entirely without mechanical pumps, water circulates naturally between the glazed solar absorber plate and the insulated storage tank via natural convection.

## 2. Economic Return on Investment
With average Caribbean sunshine exceeding 2,500 hours annually, a 200-liter solar water heater achieves full amortization within 28 to 36 months.
