# Rainwater Harvesting: Cistern Construction & Tropical Water Security

Periodic municipal water cuts and dry seasons make private rainwater harvesting an indispensable feature of Caribbean home building.

---

## 1. Sizing Your Storage Capacity
A typical tropical four-bedroom villa requires a minimum **20 to 30 m³ (20,000 to 30,000 liters)** underground reinforced concrete cistern to bridge seasonal drought periods.

## 2. Water Filtration Stages
1. **First-Flush Diverter**: Discards initial roof runoff carrying dust and organic matter.
2. **Sediment Cartridges (25 µm to 5 µm)**: Removes suspended micro-particles.
3. **UV-C Sterilization Reactor**: Neutralizes 99.9% of bacteria and pathogens without altering water taste with chemicals.
